# Homework 1: Accessible Documents and Presentations

## Learning Goals

* Familiarize yourself Adobe tools for creating accessible PDFs  
* Learn about the factors that affect the accessibility of presentation slides.

## Project Context

Everyone has various senses and abilities, many of whom do not match your own. When creating slide presentations or documents, it is important to consider how to make them accessible to everyone. In this assignment, you will learn to use Adobe Acrobat to modify PDF documents to be accessible and follow guidelines to make more accessible presentation slide decks.
