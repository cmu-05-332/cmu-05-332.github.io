# Project 5B: Project Report

## **Instructions**

Your report should include the following elements:

1. **Title of your Project**  
2. **Project Context and Goals** (0.5 \-1 pages): An analysis of the project's context and the goals. Essentially, we want to know why this project exists and why it is important.  
3. **Accessibility Critique** (1-3 pages): A description of the current state of accessibility in your selected domain. How did you determine and characterize the state of accessibility for your domain? What evidence can you provide to support your critique/determination?  
4. **Project Requirements** (1-2 pages): A description of the requirements that you agreed on with your group as executed in your project. Leave out any original project requirements that were discarded and add any new requirements that you were not aware of when you turned in P2.  
5. **Application or Solution** (1-3 pages): Your project goal was to critique accessibility and design a solution. List the user stories that went into the design here.  
    1. **User Stories.** List the user stories that helped you document and realize the functional requirements of this project. Include any wireframes, mockups, or storyboards that have influenced the final deliverable. Leave out any original user stories that were discarded and add any new user stories that you developed since turning in P2.  
    2. **Prototyping Process**. Describe your prototyping process.  
6. **Solution Testing** (1-2 pages): Write up a report summarizing the most important findings from testing your solution. This section of your report should include the following sections:  
    1. Tasks you asked the user to do  
    2. Positive aspects of the evaluation (i.e. what about your solution or application worked well)  
    3. Negative aspects of the evaluation (i.e. what kind of challenges did the users experience when trying to use your solution)  
    4. Changes you made to the design based on the lessons learned.  
7. **Solution Demo** (Video) Embed/Link a YouTube video of a 3-4 minute demo of the solution you created. Provide a voiceover narration to explain what you are showing the viewer.
8. **Links**
    1. Include links to your relevant repos and design documents (Google Drive, Github, Figma, etc.)
