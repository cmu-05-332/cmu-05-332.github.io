# Project 3: Accessibility Critique and Needfinding

## Project Context

Your project topics have been approved, and you will now begin to critique the accessibility of existing solutions, technologies, and experiences within the domain of your chosen topic. For the first portion of your group work, you will spend time evaluating the current state of accessibility and identifying unmet needs and requirements for your project/solution. This will involve background research, discussions with any available stakeholders, and experimenting with possible solutions. You will need to identify and document any existing standards you may use to assess the accessibility of your domain or the chosen application.

## Instructions

For this milestone deliverable, we would like to know how your needfinding activities are progressing. Here are the sections we expect to see in your update:

1. **Summary of Progress**  
    1. What is the problem? What makes it challenging to solve? What is your intended solution? What progress have you made so far toward designing and building that solution?  
2. **Needfinding Activities**  
    1. What specific activities have you done to learn more about your domain?

    1. Are there any standards you have found and plan to apply to evaluate accessibility in your domain? How have you evaluated accessibility so far?

    1. What are your priorities for prototyping? What will you build or create? What features do you expect it to have? Have you updated your user stories? Include any sketches, descriptions, or materials you intend to use? How do you plan to demonstrate your prototype at the end of the semester?

    1. How can we help you achieve your project goals?
