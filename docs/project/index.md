# Project Overview

For the project you will be critiquing the accessibility in a domain/topic area of your choosing, then you will prototype a redesigned solution that improves accessibility for a specific population or group of people.

The project has 6 Stages:

- Individual Idea Pitches  
- Team Formation and Team Contract  
- Group Project Proposal  
- Accessibility Critique and Needfinding  
- Prototyping/Development  
- Presentation and Demonstration

## [P0 \- Individual Idea Pitches](P0)

In this stage, you will develop a novel project idea and pitch it to the class in a 2-minute presentation. These pitches will serve to help you find teammates from the class interested in working in the same domains or on the same kind of project.

## P1 \- Team Formation and Team Contract

Once you have chosen your team members, you will come together to discuss one another's backgrounds and skills, negotiate your team norms, and write up a team contract. These include communication standards, time expectations, division of labor, a definition of equitable contribution, and procedures for managing conflicts within the team.

## P2 \- Group Project Proposal

Before your team begins work, you must first determine what you want to do. Once completed, you will come together to document the requirements in the form of user stories, UI mockup, a declaration of your technology stack, project timeline, etc. You will give a presentation in class introducing your group and your intended project.

## P3 \- Accessibility Critique and Needfinding

For the first portion of your group work you will spend time evaluating the current state of accessibility and identifying unmet needs and requirements for your project/solution. This will involve background research, discussions with available stakeholders, and experimenting with possible solutions. You will need to find and document any existing standards you may use to assess the state of accessibility in your domain or for your chosen application.

## P4 \- Prototyping/Development

During this portion of the project you will focus on building and iterating on the solution you have chosen to prototype. You will document your process and choices during this phase of the project and generate a report to share this milestone with the class.

## P5 \- Presentation and Demonstration

During the final week of course, you will present your project to the class by giving an oral presentation along with a demonstration of your final prototype. These presentations and demonstrations will likely be scheduled during final exam week.
