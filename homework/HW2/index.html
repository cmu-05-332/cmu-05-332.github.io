    Homework 2: Assistive Technology¶

    Learning Goals¶

    • Deepen your understanding of Assistive Technologies
    • Learn from real user experiences and reflect on design impact
    • Identify strengths and limitations in current ATs
    • Develop empathy and design sensitivity for accessibility challenges

    Project Context¶

    The purpose of this assignment is to:

    • Explore how people interact with Assistive Technologies (ATs)
    • Learn about a specific AT and how it supports people with disabilities
    • Reflect on both the effectiveness and limitations of the AT
    • Gain insight into lived experiences with accessibility features and devices

    You will choose one assistive technology from the list below and complete both a slide and a short written report documenting your findings and reflections.

    Getting Started¶

    1. Select one Assistive Technology. Choose your AT by setting up your slide in the shared deck.
      First come, first served: If someone has already chosen the AT you're interested in, you must choose something else. Here are some examples; You may also choose one that is not from the list:

      • Vision:
        • Screen Reader (e.g., NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver)
        • Braille Display
        • OCR Readers / Text-to-Speech Devices (e.g., KNFB Reader, Seeing AI)
        • Tactile Graphics Displays (e.g., Graphiti Display)
        • Wearable Smart Glasses (e.g., OrCam MyEye, Envision Glasses)
        • Magnification Software (e.g., ZoomText, Windows Magnifier)
      • Hearing:
        • Hearing Aid
        • Cochlear Implant
        • Induction Loop
        • Closed Captions
        • Flashing Alert Systems (for doorbells, alarms, phone calls)
      • Motor:
        • Manual Wheelchair
        • Powered Wheelchairs with Eye Gaze or Sip-and-Puff Control
        • Crutches
        • Exoskeleton
        • Adaptive Keyboards and Mice (e.g., one-handed keyboards, eye-tracking mice)
      • Cognitive and Learning
        • Reminders and Prompting Apps (e.g., Google Assistant, Pill Reminder)
        • Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) Devices (e.g., Tobii Dynavox)
      • Neurodivergence
        • Wearables for Anxiety Monitoring (e.g., Embrace2 for seizure and stress detection)
        • Fidget or Tactile Feedback Devices (for ADHD, autism)

    2. Research:

      • What is this technology?
      • Who uses it and for what purpose?
      • Find and watch videos of real people using and discussing it.
      • Take notes on what users like and dislike about it.

    3. Create Your Materials:

      • 1-slide overview for a 1–2 minute presentation during the class on Mon, Sept 8th
      • 1–2 page document with your research/analysis and reflections that you will submit to Canvas

    Instructions¶

    Part 1: Slide Presentation¶

    Warning

    Due: Monday, September 8th *in class*

    Create one slide for a brief 1–2 minute presentation of your chosen Assistive Technology. You will be asked to give the presentation during the class on Sept 8th. Note: Choose your AT by setting up your slide in the shared deck.
    First come, first served: If someone has already chosen the AT you're interested in, you must choose something else.

    Include the following:

    • Your name and AndrewID
    • Title: Name of the Assistive Technology
    • Source(s) of your content (videos, articles, etc.)
    • What is the AT?
    • Who uses it?
    • What tasks does it support?
    • Summary of a user experience video(s):
      • What do users like about it?
      • What are some frustrations or limitations?

    Part 2: Short Report¶

    Warning

    Due: Monday, September 8th *by 11:59 pm*

    Your document should have a title and include your name, the date of the assignment, and the course information (05-332/632).

    Section 1: Observation and Information Gathering¶

    Answer the following:

    • What is the AT?
    • Who is it for? Who uses it?
    • What is it intended to be used for?
    • Describe and summarize one or more videos you watched of people using or talking about this AT.
      • What was the user(s) doing?
      • What do users like or dislike about the AT?
      • What are some frustrations or limitations?
    • Include links/citations for your sources (including the videos)

    Section 2: Description and Reflection¶

    • Why did you choose this AT?
    • Describe what it was like to learn about or try out this AT (if applicable).
    • What worked well?
    • What could be improved?
    • Could the same goal be achieved in a different or better way?

    Deliverables¶

    • 1 Slide added to the shared deck (and presented in class)
    • 1–2 Page written report document (PDF or Word) submitted to canvas by midnight.

    Submit both by Monday, September 8th
